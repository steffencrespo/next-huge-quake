## Scientific guardrails
- The Bay Area 72% is the USGS Earthquake Outlook 2014-2043 (Fact Sheet 2016-3020), for the whole regional fault system. It is not a new 30-year window starting today, and it is not San Andreas only.
- Cascadia 10-15% (approximately M9, next 50 years) is from the USGS 2025 Pacific Northwest fact sheet (Fact Sheet 2025-3050); last great earthquake January 26, 1700.
- The later ~74% / 74.1% Bay Area update comes from a September 2026 SF Chronicle report with no primary technical publication yet, so it is not used anywhere.
- The two probabilities are not comparable (different faults, magnitudes, windows); no joint probability exists; "both" is a hypothetical question, not a forecast; no triggering claim is made. The title's "California vs Cascadia" is the owner's choice: the description repeats that the two are not comparable.
