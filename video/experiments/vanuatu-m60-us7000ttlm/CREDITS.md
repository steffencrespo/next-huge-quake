# Vanuatu sequence Short (USGS us7000ttlm, M6.0, 2026-10-28): local experiment credits

Title (approved): Why Does Vanuatu Keep Shaking? Another M6.0 Strikes! #shorts

Composition: `VanuatuSequence` (this folder). Narration: ElevenLabs text-to-speech (voice: Daniel), `fast-beats` pacing (Panama D4). Music: "Deified" by The Grey Room / Density & Time, YouTube Audio Library. Sound effects: synthesized for this video.

Synthetic content: the opening image (`vanuatu-coast-ai.png`, shown for about three seconds) was generated manually in ElevenLabs with GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst. It is an AI-generated illustration of a Vanuatu-style coastline. It is not a photograph or footage of the earthquakes, of Port-Olry or of any affected area (the epicenters are offshore). On screen: only a small, translucent "AI VISUALIZATION" watermark (top-left) while the image is visible, per the editorial convention in docs/DECISION_LOG.md; the full disclosure lives in the metadata, description and credits. When publishing: `containsSyntheticMedia: true` in the manifest, a sentence in the description and `.credits.txt`, and Studio disclosure. No other image is generated.

Data: U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) Earthquake Catalog, https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/ (public domain). Events: us6000u3bc, us6000u3jl, us6000u4j2, us6000u4jb (M6.3 is an mb magnitude), us7000ttlm. Epicenters are plotted at the USGS coordinates; depth is deliberately not narrated (USGS assigns 10 km to most of these events). Map: Natural Earth land (public domain), rendered with MapLibre GL. The plate cross-section is a labelled schematic.

Choices where the data is not clear-cut
- The five events are called a "sequence" only. Nothing is called an aftershock or foreshock; USGS lists no aftershock product for us7000ttlm.
- Closing: an annual-rate line was tried and dropped. Fixed ComCat bounds lat -20.5..-12.5, lon 165..171, M6.0+, 1986-2025, duplicates within 120 s merged: 256 events in 40 years = 6.4 a year, but nearby boxes give 6.2 to 7.2, so it was not narrated. Line 9 is the tectonic observation instead (the same collision built the volcanic island arc). The first draft, "no M6+ in this patch for four decades", was tested and rejected: it depends on the search radius (USGS lists an M6.8 on 2009-10-08 within 100 km of the Oct 24 epicenter), and five-in-nine-day bursts have occurred before in the region. See `data.json` (`rejectedClaim`).
- Volcanic island chain line ("That same collision built Vanuatu's chain of volcanic islands."): USGS tectonic summary of the M7.3 Espiritu Santo earthquake, us7000s8q0, 2026-03-30 (https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us7000s8q0): the Australia plate subducts at the New Hebrides Trench beneath the North Fiji Basin, and the event is in "the Vanuatu island chain". Volcanism from that subduction: Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program entries for Vanuatu volcanoes list the Vanuatu volcanic arc with tectonic setting "subduction zone" (e.g. Gaua, https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=257020; page was unreachable from the build machine on 2026-10-28 and was seen only in search results, so re-check before publishing). Secondary: Wikipedia, New Hebrides Trench and Vanuatu subduction zone.
- Plate setting: the Australian plate subducts beneath the Pacific plate at the New Hebrides (Vanuatu) Trench; published convergence rates vary strongly along the trench, so the script gives no number.
